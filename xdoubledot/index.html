AI Control System

AURORA
Adaptive Unified Real-time Operation and Regulation Algorithm

AURORA is the neural PPU controller that sits alongside the Aegis hardware — named for the Aurora Borealis, the visual signature of charged particles being guided by a magnetic field. It replaces conventional PID loops with a reinforcement-learning agent trained in simulation, learning optimal coil current policies that keep field lines grazing the BN channel walls rather than plunging into them.

Now — v1

Magnetic Shielding Control

11-dimensional observation space. SAC agent learns coil currents (Iinner, Iouter, Itrim) to maximise wall shielding, cathode protection, and stray field reduction simultaneously.

Post hardware v1 — v2

Breathing Mode Suppression

Adds discharge voltage Vd and mass flow ṁ as control outputs. Modulates Vd at 10–30 kHz to actively suppress ionisation oscillations. Anomalous electron transport black-boxed — surrogate learns the empirical mapping.

Future — v3

Full PPU Control

Adds cathode heater Iheater and keeper Ikeeper. Closes the loop on cathode lifetime. Every actuator in the PPU under unified neural control.

Simulation Outputs

Aegis HET — 3D Render. 200W Krypton Hall Effect Thruster. Iron magnetic circuit with inner solenoid (80 turns), outer solenoid (60 turns), and trim coil (40 turns). BN discharge channel with flux-surface-matched pole exit faces for magnetic shielding.
Magnetic Field — Channel Exit Zoom. Finite-difference magnetostatic solver with nonlinear Picard iteration (M19 B-H curve). Yellow streamlines show field lines crossing the N→S gap at the exit plane — the magnetic shielding condition. The field lines graze the BN walls rather than plunging into them, redirecting ions away from the ceramic surface.
