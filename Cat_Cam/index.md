# Cat Cam!

My wife, daughter, me, our two cats, and a crustation called consuela are moving country, from the UK to Estonia! We'll be moving in a recently bought van. Now, it's likely our tow lovely cats will be sleeping in the van over night whilst we're in hotels along the way (they meow constantly if we're around, and that's likely going to get us all kicked out of a hotel!) and so, we need a way to monitor them. Hence, CAT CAM!

## Cat Cam Requirements

* Remotely accessible video stream, from mobile or laptop
* Temperature measurements and alerts (sms, email, or push notifications)
* Possible CO2 monitoring, again, with alerts
* Motion detection
* Wifi or 4g connectivity, configerable
* One way audio (to list to cats)
